# Building an Exam-Prep Assistant for GMDC

I'm prototyping a study assistant for the GMDC exam focused on clarity, structure, and feedback. The challenge isn't generating answers — it's aligning explanations to the local syllabus and flagging uncertainty. I'm exploring prompt strategies that force step-by-step reasoning and reference checks, with a simple UI that feels like a flashcard app.

If you're a student or faculty member interested in a small private pilot, reach out.
